# What is ServiceNow?

ServiceNow is a powerful, cloud-based platform that automates IT and business processes. Think of it as a "platform of platforms" that helps large companies manage their digital workflows.

### Key Characteristics:

* **Single System of Record:** It provides one place to manage all IT services, requests, and incidents.
* **Automation:** It excels at automating repetitive tasks, like password resets or employee onboarding.
* **Cloud-Based:** It's accessible from anywhere with an internet connection, and you don't need to manage the underlying infrastructure.
